# Creative Feedback Layer

## Purpose

The project must not become a deterministic template generator. Canon is a
boundary; creativity remains probabilistic and resourceful inside that
boundary.

The Creative Feedback Layer adds productive resistance to repetition without
turning taste into a binary machine rule.

## Boundary

- Canon Guard owns hard structural and canonical invariants.
- Evaluator owns Core decision policy.
- Creative Feedback produces advisory observations about novelty and mechanism reuse.
- The provider proposes candidate content; it never decides canon.

Creative Feedback never mutates the candidate and never invents canon.

## Reuse has two meanings

1. Identity reuse: a canonical asset can recur when its intention remains explicit.
2. Mechanism reuse: repeating the same causal comic construction can make a new
   gag feel derivative even when its visible prop changes.

The first live Qwen experiment exposed the second case: replacing jamón with
chorizo while preserving essentially the same training/strike/reaction structure
is not meaningful novelty.

## Resourcefulness

The generation principle is:

> Tienes estas reglas del universo. Ahora encuentra una solución que todavía no
> hayas visto.

The system therefore asks the generator to preserve canon while searching for
a different causal chain. A prop substitution is not enough. Consequence,
misdirection, inversion, role switching and environmental interaction are
resources, not mandatory recipes.

## Feedback loop

After a candidate is evaluated, Core derives bounded creative guidance for the
next iteration. A high-confidence mechanism reuse finding requests another
generation rather than silently accepting the derivative candidate.

The feedback does not score the candidate and does not decide aesthetic taste.
It explains why another pass is requested and gives a direction without
prescribing the final gag.

## Current creative memory

`data/creative_mechanisms.json` currently contains one explicit mechanism record
derived from the current semantic Gag 001 documentation. The memory is
deliberately small and authoritative. Historical images are not automatically
converted into creative rules; they should be extracted deliberately and
revalidated before being promoted into the creative memory.
