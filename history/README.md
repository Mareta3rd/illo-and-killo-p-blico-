# History

## Purpose
Keep the origin, evolution, milestones, legacy, and historical creative corpus of the project in one place.

## Files
- `ORIGIN.md` — earliest source of the project.
- `EVOLUTION.md` — progression from early pieces to canon.
- `LEGACY.md` — long-term decisions and lessons.
- `MILESTONES.md` — major turning points.
- `creative-corpus/MANIFEST.md` — recovered historical images from Arsa & Pisha Origins and Killo & Illo, explicitly non-canonical.

## Rule
History records the path taken by the project; it does not replace canon.

Historical creative material may preserve successful ideas, jokes, compositions, visual mechanisms, or lessons without preserving the obsolete character design or terminology in which they first appeared. Reuse requires comparison against the current Arsa & Pisha canon.
